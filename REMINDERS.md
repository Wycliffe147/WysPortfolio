# Future Improvements & Roadmap

## 1. Client Quotes & Testimonial Attribution
- Add explicit personal attribution for each client quote:
  - Full name of the person who gave the testimonial (e.g. Pastor, Academic Director, Retail Manager, Agribusiness Founder).
  - Exact job title or institutional role.
  - Organization badge or avatar representation.
  - Experiment with layout options to see whether displaying "Name, Title at Organization" or a dedicated spokesperson card looks best visually.

## 2. Mobile UI / UX Optimization
- Audit navigation menu transitions and ensure all touch targets meet or exceed 44x44 pixels.
- Refine card padding, margins, and horizontal spacing on small screen viewports (under 380px).
- Verify interactive story dialogs for smooth scrolling and intuitive close buttons on mobile devices.
- Review the hero deployment status card and sparkline chart for clean vertical flow on mobile phones.
